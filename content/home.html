Who We Are

Comprehensive Aviation Supply & Support Solutions

AMMAC Aircraft Parts delivers a complete portfolio of aviation products and services designed to maximize fleet availability and operational readiness. With strategically positioned inventory in Dubai and Singapore, a global supplier network, and access to certified repair facilities, we provide responsive solutions that maximize aircraft availability and reduce operational downtime.
